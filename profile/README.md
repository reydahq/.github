# Reyda

[Reyda](https://reyda.ai) is an AI bid manager and tender management workspace for companies selling to government and responding to private RFPs.

Teams use Reyda to:

- Find and qualify relevant public tenders.
- Review tender documents and requirements.
- Draft evidence-backed proposals and complete forms with AI.
- Coordinate owners, deadlines, and submissions in one pipeline.

Reyda monitors more than 4,000 procurement sources across the EU, the UK, and Switzerland, including TED Europa, Find a Tender, and national, regional, and specialist portals.
