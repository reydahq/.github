# Reyda

[Reyda](https://reyda.ai) is the workspace where teams work with Emma, their organization-owned AI bid manager and business developer.

Instead of giving teams another workflow to operate, Reyda lets colleagues delegate commercial objectives in ordinary language. Emma brings together company knowledge, opportunity context, documents, decisions, and ongoing work—then keeps responsibility for the outcome.

Emma can help an organization:

- Monitor markets, buyers, opportunities, deadlines, and changes.
- Investigate and qualify tenders, RFPs, grants, and other commercial opportunities.
- Turn an objective into research, plans, evidence-backed deliverables, and coordinated work.
- Keep independent work moving while bringing people the decisions and approvals only they can make.
- Preserve context, progress, files, and outstanding decisions across conversations and time.
- Finish with a clear, verifiable handoff: what is complete, what needs review, and what remains blocked.

Public procurement is Emma's initial professional focus. Reyda monitors more than 4,000 procurement sources across the EU, the UK, and Switzerland, while supporting the wider business-development and contracting work around each opportunity.
