# Reyda

[Reyda](https://reyda.ai) builds Emma, an AI bid manager for SMEs. Give Emma a tender or RFP and she carries the work from opportunity through response readiness—researching the buyer, mapping requirements, finding company evidence, preparing response material, identifying gaps, and keeping the bid moving.

Reyda is the connected workspace that gives Emma the opportunity context, buyer documents, company knowledge, approved evidence, response work, and decisions she needs to do the job.

## Give Emma the tender

- **Opportunity brief:** buyer, scope, deadline, fit, risks, and pursuit recommendation.
- **Requirement map:** what the buyer asks for, where it appears, and what the response needs.
- **Evidence:** relevant company experience, policies, certificates, and reusable material—with gaps made visible.
- **Prepared response material:** proposal sections, plans, forms, questionnaires, tables, and supporting documents.
- **Focused decisions:** the research, source material, consequences, and recommendation people need to exercise company authority.
- **Readiness check:** missing items, inconsistent claims, unresolved approvals, version issues, and submission instructions.

People retain buyer relationships, commercial judgment, pricing, formal commitments, signatures, and final submission authority. Emma carries the research, preparation, coordination, and routine checking around those decisions.

Reyda monitors more than 4,000 procurement sources across the EU, the UK, and Switzerland. Teams can also bring private RFPs and RFQs into the same workspace.
